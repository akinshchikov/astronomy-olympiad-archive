# v0.6.0 — Global astronomy olympiad source expansion

## Highlights

- Broader international coverage with bounded, source-specific discovery.
- Coverage expanded to 13 additional olympiad families and 940 additional indexed
  physical files.
- Safe local ZIP/container expansion, URL path encoding, and response-signature
  validation before accepting downloaded files.
- Resumable validated download checkpoints that retain current discovery metadata.
- Terminal outcomes for accessible, discovery-only, unresolved, and deferred sources.
- Preservation of source roles and family boundaries throughout discovery,
  normalization, and public indices.

## Source-access outcomes

The source audit evaluated 32 candidates during this release: 13 produced partial
indexed coverage, 11 remained metadata-only, 7 were unresolved, and 1 was deferred
pending a reliable archive. These are source-access outcomes, not classifications of
the olympiads. Representative constraints include robots blocking, form gates,
external Drive policy blocking, no safely enumerable archive, mirror-only provenance,
and lack of a reliable archive.

## Publishing policy

This GitHub release contains code and lightweight metadata only. Downloaded
olympiad PDF, ZIP, DOC, and DOCX binaries remain local and ignored; no mirrored
corpus is attached. INAO/HBCSE no-redistribution restrictions remain respected.
